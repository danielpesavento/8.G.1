Rigid Motions Sandbox

Draw a shape, drag it, then translate, reflect, or rotate it

California CCSS Math 8.G.1 · Interactive Construction Tool

How to use this:
  1. Click Draw new shape, then click 3 or 4 points on the grid to build your own triangle or quadrilateral.
  2. Drag any labeled point to reshape the blue original or the gold image at any time.
  3. Use the panel on the right to translate, reflect, or rotate the gold image. Watch the measurement table — lengths and angles always match.
